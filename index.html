US vs. UK Investor Matrix

Key regulatory, tax, and instrument differences every transatlantic investor must know.

Feature 🇺🇸 United States 🇬🇧 United Kingdom Strategic Takeaway
Primary Tax-Advantaged Account 401(k) ($23,500/yr limit) & Roth IRA ($7,000/yr) Stocks & Shares ISA (£20,000/yr allowance) UK ISA has no lockup until retirement, whereas US 401(k) penalizes withdrawals before 59½.
Capital Gains Tax (CGT) 0%, 15%, or 20% depending on income brackets 10% or 20% (shares) / 24% (property) after £3,000 allowance UK investors must maximize ISAs to shelter profits from the shrinking CGT threshold.
Spot Crypto ETFs Fully SEC-approved (IBIT, FBTC, etc.) ETNs allowed for professional investors; retail restricted by FCA US retail investors enjoy direct brokerage access to spot Bitcoin & Ethereum ETFs.
Forex Trading Leverage Max 1:50 on major pairs (CFTC / NFA regulations) Max 1:30 for retail under FCA rules; negative balance protection Both jurisdictions strictly protect retail traders from toxic 1:500 offshore leverage.
Forex Spread Betting Not legal under US tax code 100% Tax-Free (classified as betting, exempt from CGT & Stamp Duty) UK residents can trade FX via spread betting completely tax-free.
